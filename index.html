WordPress vs. Shopify: Which One Is Right for You?

Both platforms work well, but they fit different kinds of businesses. If you're still confused after reading this, here's the short version: selling products online → Shopify. Everything else → WordPress.

What You're Comparing WordPress Shopify
What it's built for A general-purpose website: services, information, blog, bookings An online store: products, cart, checkout
Monthly cost No platform fee. You only pay for hosting (~$3-10/month) Shopify charges its own monthly subscription on top of my build cost: Basic $22/mo, Grow $58/mo, Advanced $359/mo, Plus from $2,300/mo
Who manages it day to day Slightly more setup, but you or your staff can update pages easily after a short training Very easy. Built so store owners can add products and manage orders themselves
Selling products online Possible with WooCommerce, but needs extra setup Built for this from day one. Smoothest checkout experience
Ranking on Google (SEO) Best SEO control. Ideal if you want to rank for services, blog content, or local search Good SEO, but less flexible than WordPress for content-heavy ranking strategies
Best for Clinics, academies, consultants, service businesses, real estate, and any site that needs strong content and local SEO Textile and garment brands, retailers, and anyone selling products online

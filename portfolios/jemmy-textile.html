Project Description

THE STORY

Jemmy Textile manufactures home textiles, denim fabrics, towels, hospitality fabrics, and baby products for international markets.

The company needed a professional website that could present product categories clearly and attract wholesale buyers from around the world.

OUR APPROACH

  • Structured Product Presentation: Developed product sections that organize textile categories for easy browsing.
  • Inquiry-Based Lead Generation: Integrated product inquiry forms so international buyers can request quotations.
  • SEO Optimization: Implemented Yoast SEO with structured metadata and SEO-friendly URLs.
  • Performance Improvements: Optimized images and implemented caching for faster page loading.

RESULTS

The website now functions as an international product catalog and lead generation platform.

Project Description

THE STORY

ShopGotical sells replacement filters compatible with refrigerators and air purification systems from brands such as Samsung, LG, GE, Kenmore, and Bosch.

Customers often struggle to identify the correct filter for their appliance. The goal was to create a website where users could easily find compatible replacement filters and complete purchases confidently.

OUR APPROACH

  • WooCommerce Product Structure: Organized filters based on appliance brand and compatibility.
  • Comparison Tools: Implemented product comparison and wishlist functionality using YITH WooCommerce plugins.
  • Order Tracking Integration: Added shipment tracking using the AfterShip plugin.
  • Conversion Optimization: Highlighted product ratings, shipping offers, and trust signals to improve purchase confidence.

RESULTS

The store now provides a clear shopping experience for customers searching for compatible replacement filters.
